About La Letizia

Our Story

A sanctuary of specialty coffee, great conversations, and unforgettable nights.

Nestled on the 5th Floor at 70 Beck Bagan Row, Park Circus, Ballygunge, Kolkata — La Letizia is your go-to destination for hand-crafted espresso drinks, artisanal café bites, and a late-night lounge vibe that keeps the city awake until 5:00 AM. Whether you're catching up with friends or unwinding after a long day, La Letizia delivers an experience that lingers long after the last sip.
